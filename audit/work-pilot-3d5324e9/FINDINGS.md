**Answers to the seven questions**

1. **Minting channel.** No other path mints. Rights are nonzero only for the pilot and only between the assignment inside pay() and the vault's consumeRights; a revert in earn reverts the batch; the nonReentrant guard and the post-earn check that nothing remains authorised close re-entry; imdUSD transfers carry no hooks. Confirmed by the fork test and my duplicate-in-batch test.
2. **Caps.** No way to exceed EPOCH_CAP or LIFETIME_CAP, pay beyond an epoch's total, pay a share twice, pay a vetoed epoch, or count a window twice. The inverse problem is real: an unpayable epoch keeps its total committed forever and burns lifetime capacity (low).
3. **Leaf encoding** matches OpenZeppelin StandardMerkleTree exactly. Double hashing stops internal nodes posing as leaves, and the epoch inside the leaf stops cross-epoch reuse.
4. **Bindings.** A reverting or gas-burning adapter or token is skipped per share as designed, and return-bombing is bounded by the stipend. A malformed Binding word from the adapter reverts the whole batch, and the adapter is an ERC-1967 proxy owned outside the protocol Safe (low). The 60k stipend suffices for the real seat collection.
5. **Compromise cases.** Reading the governor live is sound since Parameters.governor() is pure. A compromised poster is capped, except that an unbounded toBlock permanently bricks propose() (low). Two window rules are wrong: gaps are accepted and lost forever, and a narrowed recount vetoed twice loses blocks (both low).
6. **Install and exit.** Installation at wage 0 works on the live fork. Wage 1 only opens earn; earnLine, redemption and the old SwarmWorkOracle's claim behave as documented. Exit strands every unpaid share, and no in-repo successor can replace the pilot after its first mint (info).
7. **Builder.** Counting, rounding and document hashing are correct and fail-safe. It does not filter on the on-chain Completed status, relying on the live API to exclude failed requests (verified: two status-2 completions in the dry-run window name oracle requests). It silently skips paid requests on API 404, non-attested status, or a Requested event older than the 50,000-block lookback, and never checks --from or --epoch against the chain (low).

**Specialist findings dropped or merged.** None were dropped as false. The 27 specialist items collapsed into 10: four copies each of the toBlock, unpayable-epoch, status-filter and exit-path findings, three of the silent-skip family, two each of the contiguity and adapter findings. The lookback skip merged into the silent-skip finding. No critical or high findings exist, so only the toBlock finding carries a proof.

## 1. [low] propose() does not bound toBlock; toBlock = type(uint64).max (or any far-future block) permanently disables every later propose()
`src/PaidOraclePilot.sol:183`

propose() only checks `toBlock < fromBlock`. It never requires toBlock <= block.number or toBlock < type(uint64).max. If the poster (mistaken or compromised) posts toBlock = type(uint64).max and the governor misses the 24 h veto window, every later propose() evaluates `last.toBlock + 1` in checked uint64 arithmetic and reverts with Panic(0x11) for every caller, with any arguments, forever. setPoster() does not help (the stuck epoch stays `last`), veto() is closed, and because the pilot exposes no predecessor(), once it has minted Parameters._validate accepts no successor that exists in the repository. The weaker form: any toBlock past the chain head (verified: on a chain at block 100, propose(.., 1, 1_000_000) is accepted) forces every later window to start after it, so paid work completed in the intervening blocks can never be counted. No funds are at risk beyond that epoch's capped total; the pilot's remaining lifetime budget is dead. Merged from four specialists (economics, permissions, flow, math). Fix: require toBlock <= block.number (and toBlock < type(uint64).max) in propose(), and/or compute the floor in uint256.

**Reproduction.** 1) poster: propose(bytes32(1), 1e18, 1, type(uint64).max, '') -> accepted (epoch 0). 2) warp 7 days (veto window missed). 3) governor: setPoster(newPoster); newPoster: propose(bytes32(1), 1e18, 1, 2, '') -> reverts Panic(0x11) arithmetic overflow at line 183 instead of WindowNotAfterPrevious; every later propose reverts the same way. Expected: step 1 refused, or step 3 answered with the contract's own error. Reproduced: the attached test fails on the current code with `0x4e487b71 != 0x302d5662` (Panic selector vs WindowNotAfterPrevious). Also test/scratch/PilotReview.t.sol::test_futureToBlockAccepted shows propose(.., 1, 1_000_000) accepted at block 100.

## 2. [low] An epoch whose shares can never be paid keeps its total in totalCommitted forever and consumes LIFETIME_CAP; nothing releases it after the 24 h veto window
`src/PaidOraclePilot.sol:197`

totalCommitted rises by `total` at posting and is lowered only by veto(), which is refused once 24 h have passed. The posted total is not tied to the leaves, and no function retires an epoch's unpaid remainder (`total - paid`) later. Concrete ways an epoch becomes partly or wholly unpayable after the window: the builder hashes leaves with the operator-supplied --epoch (pilot/build-tally.mjs line 35) and never reads epochCount(), so a wrong --epoch produces a root for which every pay() reverts BadProof; a mistyped root; agents whose binding is ERC-1155/6909 or absent resolve to Unpayable (and if the agent never binds an ERC-721, the share is committed forever); a tree whose leaves sum above `total` makes the last shares revert OverEpochTotal. Each such epoch burns up to 350 of the 3,000 imdUSD lifetime, one 6-day slot and its block window (the floor moves past its toBlock, so the paid work in it cannot be recounted). Merged from four specialists. Fix: let the governor (or anyone after a long grace) release `total - paid` of an epoch back from totalCommitted and let the floor fall back to it; and make build-tally.mjs read epochCount() and refuse a mismatching --epoch.

**Reproduction.** State: epochCount()==0. Poster posts root = keccak256(bytes.concat(keccak256(abi.encode(uint256(1), 7, 350e18)))) (a tree built with --epoch 1) with total 350e18, window 100..200. Warp 24 h: pay(0, [7], [350e18], [[]]) -> BadProof. Governor: veto(0) -> VetoWindowClosed. totalCommitted()==350e18 with totalPaid()==0 and stays so: after seven further 350e18 epochs totalCommitted==2_800e18 and propose(.., 200e18+1, ..) reverts OverLifetimeCap, so 350 imdUSD of lifetime capacity is gone with nothing minted. Reproduced in test/scratch/PilotReview.t.sol::test_unpayableEpochKeepsLifetimeCapForever (passes on the current code = defect present).

## 3. [low] Window contiguity is not enforced: propose() accepts fromBlock > floor and the builder never checks --from/--epoch against the chain, so skipped blocks are lost forever
`src/PaidOraclePilot.sol:184`

The README says --from must be the previous epoch's toBlock + 1, but propose() only refuses fromBlock < floor, and pilot/build-tally.mjs takes --epoch, --from and --to as bare operator arguments (line 35) without reading epochCount() or epochs(n-1).toBlock (it does not know the pilot's address). A typo or off-by-one in --from posts a window with a hole; because every later epoch must start after this one's toBlock, Intake Completed events in the hole can never be counted and that paid work is never bonused. Merged from two specialists (permissions, flow). Fix: require fromBlock == floor in propose() (the vetoed-recount case keeps floor = last.fromBlock), and have the builder derive --from and --epoch from the contract.

**Reproduction.** 1) poster: propose(root, 1e18, 100, 200, '') -> epoch 0. 2) warp 6 days; propose(root, 1e18, 300, 400, '') -> accepted although blocks 201..299 were never counted. 3) warp 6 days; propose(root, 1e18, 201, 299, '') -> reverts WindowNotAfterPrevious. Expected: step 2 refused (non-contiguous) or step 3 accepted. Reproduced in test/scratch/PilotReview.t.sol::test_gapBetweenWindowsAcceptedAndUnrecoverable.

## 4. [low] Floor after two consecutive vetoes is the last vetoed epoch's fromBlock, so a narrowed recount that is vetoed again loses blocks no live epoch ever counted
`src/PaidOraclePilot.sol:183`

The floor for the next window looks only at the LAST epoch. One veto is handled (floor = last.fromBlock). But if a vetoed window is recounted with a NARROWER window and that recount is also vetoed, the floor becomes the narrower fromBlock, and the blocks between the first vetoed window's start and the narrower start can never be counted by any epoch, although no live epoch ever covered them. Paid work completed there is permanently unpayable. (Non-overlap itself holds: every fromBlock is >= every earlier live epoch's toBlock + 1.) Reported by the permissions specialist. Fix: compute the floor as max(toBlock + 1) over all non-vetoed epochs, i.e. the earliest fromBlock of the trailing run of vetoed epochs.

**Reproduction.** 1) propose(root, 1e18, 100, 200) -> epoch 0 live. 2) warp 6 d; propose(root, 1e18, 201, 300) -> epoch 1; governor veto(1). 3) warp 6 d; propose(root, 1e18, 250, 300) -> epoch 2 (floor 201, accepted); governor veto(2). 4) warp 6 d; propose(root, 1e18, 201, 300). Expected: accepted (201..300 never covered live). Actual: reverts WindowNotAfterPrevious because floor = epochs[2].fromBlock = 250; blocks 201..249 are lost for good. Reproduced in test/scratch/PilotReview.t.sol::test_floorAfterNarrowedRecountLosesBlocks.

`src/PaidOraclePilot.sol:250`

The NatSpec promises that a lookup that fails leaves only that share unpaid. The staticcall and the `data.length < 96` check cover reverts and short answers, but abi.decode of a struct with uint8 and address fields reverts when a word carries bits above the type's width, and that revert is outside any try/staticcall guard, so pay() reverts for every agent in the batch (and for every batch containing that agent). Verified on mainnet with cast: ADAPTER 0xde152AfB...dD336 has 329 bytes of code, its ERC-1967 implementation slot holds 0xa6d23f27d3b1780b12488482a008cb3c3787135f and owner() is 0x03302Df40186D9B85faEA4fbb6cC5da028B23149 (not the protocol Safe). Its upgrader therefore decides every recipient (bindingOf is the only payee source, with no delay or sanity check) and can stall pay() entirely with a malformed word; a reverting or gas-burning bindingOf or ownerOf, by contrast, is correctly skipped (verified), and return-bombing is bounded by the 60k stipend. For the live code every real agent resolves (fork test: 64 real agents, ~68k gas each, 60k stipend sufficient for the seat collection). Merged from flow and math specialists. Fix: decode the three words manually (abi.decode(data, (uint256, uint256, uint256)) then range-check standard <= 255 and tokenContract < 2^160, else return address(0)); document the adapter admin as a trusted party in the README.

**Reproduction.** Epoch with leaves (0, 1, 1e18) and (0, 2, 2e18); agent 2 bound to an ERC-721 seat. vm.mockCall(ADAPTER, abi.encodeCall(IPilotAdapter.bindingOf, (1)), abi.encode(uint256(256), uint256(uint160(seat)), uint256(11))) -> pay(0, [1,2], [1e18,2e18], proofs) reverts (abi.decode panic) instead of emitting Unpayable(0, 1) and paying agent 2. Same with the tokenContract word (1<<160 | seat). With vm.mockCallRevert on bindingOf(1) instead, pay() pays 2e18 and skips agent 1 as documented. Reproduced in test/scratch/PilotReview.t.sol::test_malformedBindingWordRevertsWholeBatch, ::test_malformedAddressWordRevertsWholeBatch, ::test_revertingBindingIsSkippedNotFatal.

## 6. [low] Builder ignores the on-chain Completed status; refused/failed requests (status 1/2) pass stage 1 and are excluded only by the live API's status string
`pilot/build-tally.mjs:147`

Stage 1 records `status` but never filters on it. Intake v2 completes a request with status 0 (answered), 1 (refused) or 2 (ended without a result), and the fee is not refunded in the failure cases. A status-2 Completed whose uri names an oracle request passes the uri regex and the IMD-payment test and becomes a candidate; it is then dropped only because the live API answers a status other than 'attested' (line 165) and the frozen document carries no matching acceptance. The README states the live API is used for one thing only (job lookup); in fact the 'exclude refused' guarantee rests on it. If the plane closes an Intake request with status 2 at its deadline and later attests the job (API 'attested', frozen document naming the request), the builder pays the 1.5 imdUSD bonus for a request whose payer received no answer, which the brief says must be excluded. Merged from four specialists. Fix: `if (c.args.status !== 0) { skip(`completed with status ${c.args.status}`); continue; }` before the API lookup.

**Reproduction.** Verified live (Tenderly RPC) in the dry-run window 26194146..26215746 on Intake 0xa43e6F75ee006411F79Ac1C84120606C2330DE82: 117 Completed events, statuses {0:113, 1:1, 2:3}; two status-2 completions name oracle requests 39cda5f9-453c-4bfa-8f84-d592fb707994 (tx 0x58ae424d15e8887cea1e59324c0687932a3e6bd034f9049b04d5d19b8c3c4197) and 0d6f8aa9-4738-447d-90cf-7cd7dcfbeac9 (tx 0xa65daf69f3570764f1654225f3caf8c8010bb781d0d10aae1503220b4888917b). ## 8. [info] Exit path: after the wage returns to 0 or a successor is applied, every posted-but-unpaid share is stranded; the pilot has no predecessor(), so after its first mint neither address(0) nor another Paid
`src/PaidOraclePilot.sol:238`

pay() mints only through vault.earn, which reads oracle().mintingRights(msg.sender). Installation is sound: Parameters._validate accepts the pilot at wage 0 (vault() matches, mintingRights(vault) answers 0, totalEarned == 0 and the live SwarmWorkOracle's totalCredited == 0), wage 1 only opens earn, earnLine and redemption are unchanged, and SwarmWorkOracle.claim then reverts NotTheVaultsOracle (fork test 9/9 reproduced live via Tenderly). But the exit is harsher than the README says: at wage 0 earn reverts WorkMintingOff; once a successor is applied the vault reads the successor's mintingRights(pilot) == 0 and earn reverts InsufficientRights. So shares posted but not yet paid (feeds stale through the window, Unpayable agents who bind later, batches nobody submitted) can never be paid, contradicting setPoster's 'epochs already past their window stay payable' and the README's 'stays payable'. Replacement needs a successor answering predecessor() == pilot (Parameters.sol line 431), which no contract in the repository does, and proposeWorkOracle(address(0)) is refused once totalEarned != 0, so the vault's own SwarmWorkOracle is unreachable after the first paid batch. The pilot exposes no view a successor could use to carry remaining shares. Merged from four specialists. Not a bypass: document the exit order (setPoster(0), wait out the last window, pay every batch, then propose) and give the successor type a way to import epochs()/paidOut.

**Reproduction.** 1) Pilot installed at wage 1; epoch 0 posted (total 3e18) with a leaf for agent U who has no binding; after 24 h pay() emits Unpayable(0, U) and pays the rest. 2) Governor: proposeWage(0), 48 h, applyPending(). 3) U binds an ERC-721 seat; pay(0, [U], [amt], [proof]) -> vault.earn reverts WorkMintingOff. 4) Governor: proposeWorkOracle(address(0)) -> InvalidWorkOracle (totalEarned != 0); proposeWorkOracle(new PaidOraclePilot(vault, poster)) -> InvalidWorkOracle (predecessor() probe returns 0). 5) With any successor S applied and wage 1: pay(0, [U], ...) -> S.mintingRights(pilot) == 0 -> InsufficientRights. epochs(0).total - epochs(0).paid stays in totalCommitted forever.

## 10. [info] Builder checks attestation.requestId only on oracleAcceptance[0] while crediting every entry's agentId, and does not deduplicate two Intake requests naming the same oracle request
`pilot/build-tally.mjs:176`

Line 176 reads the attestation from the first acceptance entry only, and line 184 credits every entry's agentId. A frozen document carrying acceptance entries for more than one attestation (a re-run or merged record) would have all its agents paid for a request only the first entry answers. Reported in part by the flow specialist. Fix: require every entry's attestation.requestId to match (or credit only matching entries), and skip a second Completed naming an oracleId already counted.

**Reproduction.** Frozen document with oracleAcceptance = [{agentId 1, document.oracle.attestation.requestId A}, {agentId 2, ... requestId B}] listed in the day receipt for job J, live API naming J for request A: agents 1 and 2 are both credited for A.
